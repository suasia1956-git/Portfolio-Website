How I test accessibility

  1. Check the HTML outline: one h1, headings in order, and landmarks for every region.
  2. Put the mouse away and tab through the page, confirming a visible focus ring and a logical order.
  3. Run Lighthouse and axe to catch contrast, label and naming issues.
  4. Listen to the page with a screen reader and fix anything confusing or missing.
  5. Zoom to 200% and test at a 320px width to confirm nothing is cut off.
